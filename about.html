About Rene's Brew

A place where coffee, food, and good company come together.

Our Story

Rene's Brew is a student-created coffee shop website designed to provide customers with a simple and enjoyable way to explore coffee, food, and other delicious treats.

Our goal is to create a cozy digital coffee shop experience where customers can browse the menu, add products to their cart, place an order, and track its status.

This website was created as a group project to demonstrate our knowledge of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
